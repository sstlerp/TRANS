# User guide (workflows)

**Navigation (same structure as SSTL-ERP):** after signing in, the **Main Dashboard** shows one card per module
(Fleet, Drivers, Compliance, Finance, Fuel, Toll, Tyres …). Click a module card to see the cards of its screens, then
click a screen card to open it. The header always has **Main Dashboard**, a button back to the current module, alerts,
change password and **Sign out**. Quick search (vehicle, driver, tyre, invoice, UTR …) is on the Main Dashboard.
The executive dashboard is under **Dashboard → Executive Dashboard**.

**Data-entry screens (same layout as SSTL-ERP):** a dark toolbar with the screen title and **New / Edit / Save /
Cancel**; below it the summary bar (mode, record ID, name, status, quick actions — ⌄ shows the full record summary);
then the entry form — the first section across the full width, the other sections two per row — and the table of
records at the bottom. The app header, toolbar, summary bar and the table's search bar stay pinned while you scroll,
and table column headings stay visible inside the table. A blank form is ready when the screen opens — fill it and
press **Save**; the form clears for the next entry and the new row is highlighted. Click any row to load it into the
form, change it and press **Update** (or **Cancel**). The table has search (partial or *exact*), filters, date range,
sorting (click headers), pagination and Excel/CSV/PDF/print.
Row menu (⋯): actions, **Documents**, **Audit history**, activate/deactivate, delete (soft). Dates are typed and shown
as **DD/MM/YYYY** (time as HH:MM).

## 1. First-time setup (Administration & masters)

1. **Organization** → Companies, Branches, Departments, Locations (godowns, workshops, warehouses), States/Districts/RTOs.
2. **Administration → Master Configuration (Lookups)**: adjust dropdown values (vehicle types/statuses, ownership,
   document types, contract types, insurance policy types …).
3. **Fleet → Vehicle Categories / Sub-categories / Sub-category Attributes** (e.g. Truck → LPG with Tank Capacity,
   Axle Count …). **Cost Centers**: add non-vehicle ones (Administration, Workshop, Common …).
4. **Tyres → Positions / Vehicle Tyre Layouts / Locations**; **Fuel → Fuel Types / Stations / Cards**;
   **Toll → Plazas**; **Finance → Banks / Bank Accounts / Credit Types / Expense Types / Matching Rules**.
5. **Compliance → Renewal Types / Renewal Rules** (e.g. PESO for LPG sub-category, 2 certificates).
6. **Administration → System Settings / Rules**: tolerances, reminder days, odometer policy, partial allocation,
   income basis, common-cost allocation basis, tyre cost treatment. **Approval / Notification rules**, **Users & Roles**.

## 2. Vehicles

*New* → identity, category + sub-category (technical attributes appear for the chosen sub-category), branch, make,
status, capacity, tyre layout, opening odometer, **Fuel Configuration** (one or more fuel types, one primary).
On save the vehicle's **cost center** is created and renewal slots are generated from the renewal rules.
Category/sub-category/cost center are locked afterwards: use **Change Category / Cost Center** with an effective date
and reason — history is preserved and reports use the classification valid on each transaction date.
**Record Odometer** logs readings with chronology checks (lower readings need an authorised override with reason).

## 3. Compliance & insurance

* **Renewal Dashboard / Calendar**: due today / ≤7 / ≤15 / ≤30 days, expired, renewed, pending documents/payment,
  per category tabs and a month calendar.
* **Renewals**: one record per certificate; multiple slots per vehicle (e.g. *PESO #1*, *PESO #2*). Use **Renew** to
  create the next certificate — the old one becomes RENEWED and stays in history. Attach the certificate via Documents.
* **Insurance**: several concurrent policies per vehicle (vehicle, driver, CLL, PLI …), **Renew Policy** keeps history.
* Reminders are generated by the daily job at the configured days (per rule/slot or the global default) — see Alerts.

## 4. Contracts, invoices & receipts

Contracts hold customer, dates, billing method/rate and **vehicle allocation %**. Invoices (incl. partial, advance,
credit note) must allocate their taxable income to vehicles/cost centers (auto-filled from the contract split).
Income is recognised from invoices; bank receipts only settle them (Receivables report shows ageing).

**GST e-invoice:** each invoice has an *E-Invoice (GST)* section. Generate the e-invoice on the GST e-invoice portal
(or through your GSP), then enter the **IRN** (64 characters), acknowledgement number and date — the status becomes
*Generated*. From then on the customer, date and amounts are locked; **Cancel E-Invoice (IRN)** is allowed within 24
hours of the acknowledgement (cancel it on the portal too), otherwise issue a credit note. Set the business rule
`EINVOICE_APPLICABLE` to *true* if your turnover requires e-invoicing — new invoices then start as *Pending* and are
listed under **Contracts → E-Invoice Pending**.

## 4a. Trips & freight, e-way bills, driver advances & settlements

Module card **Trips & Freight**.

* **Trips / Lorry Receipts** — one record per consignment: LR number and date, customer (bill-to), vehicle, driver,
  route (from/to, PIN, state, distance), consignor/consignee with GSTIN, goods (HSN, packages, weight, value, the
  consignor's invoice) and freight. Freight is *fixed*, *per tonne* or *per km* (calculated as you type) plus
  loading, unloading, detention and other charges, less the advance received from the customer. Driver-paid and
  company-paid trip expenses (loading, cash toll, police/RTO, parking …) are entered in the grid.
  Work it with the actions: **Start Trip** (start time and odometer; stops if the vehicle is already on a trip;
  warns about an expired licence and a missing e-way bill) → **Mark Delivered** (end odometer → actual km;
  per-km freight is recalculated; the trip's e-way bills are closed) → **POD Received** (attach the scanned POD under
  Documents) → **Create Invoice** (raises the customer invoice for the total freight, allocated to the vehicle) →
  **Close Trip**. Planned or running trips can be cancelled with a reason. Once invoiced, customer, vehicle and
  freight are locked. The links above the form open the trip's e-way bills, the **E-way JSON** download for the GST
  portal, advances and settlement.
* **E-way Bills** — enter the 12-digit number and generation time against the trip; distance, goods value, places and
  document are copied from the trip. Validity is calculated: 1 day per 200 km (over-dimensional cargo 20 km),
  ending at midnight. **Extend Validity** (remaining km or a new time, with reason), **Change Vehicle (Part-B)**,
  **Close**, **Cancel** (only within 24 hours of generation). The list is sorted by expiry; alerts are raised before a
  bill expires while the trip is in transit. Business rules: `EWAY_BILL_THRESHOLD` (₹50,000), `EWAY_CHECK_MODE`
  (WARN / ERROR / IGNORE at trip start), `EWAY_KM_PER_DAY`, `EWAY_ODC_KM_PER_DAY`, `EWAY_ALERT_HOURS`.
* **Driver Advances** — cash / UPI given to a driver (optionally for a trip). Warns above `DRIVER_ADVANCE_LIMIT`;
  alerts for advances open longer than `ADVANCE_ALERT_DAYS`. An advance that is not yet in a settlement can be
  cancelled.
* **Driver Settlements (trip sheet)** — choose the driver and a trip (or leave the trip blank to settle everything
  up to the date / in the period) and save: the driver's open advances and driver-paid trip expenses are pulled in.
  **Net = driver-paid expenses + allowance (days × rate, `DRIVER_ALLOWANCE_PER_DAY`) + other earnings − deductions −
  advances**; positive = pay the driver, negative = recover from the driver. **Approve** (permission `trip.approve`)
  settles the advances; **Mark Paid / Recovered**; **Cancel** reopens the advances and expenses. The bank payment or
  recovery can be linked in the Reconciliation Workbench (**Match driver advance / settlement / recovery**).

Money is counted once: freight income only through the invoice; trip expenses are cost of the vehicle's cost center;
advances are not cost (they are recovered in the settlement); the approved allowance is driver cost.

## 5. Bank statements & reconciliation

1. Import the statement (Imports → Import Wizard, type BANK, choose the account) or add a manual transaction.
2. Every credit starts **UNCLASSIFIED**. Open **Finance → Reconciliation Workbench**:
   * **Credits**: match invoice(s) (partial, many invoices, or several receipts for one invoice), contract income /
     advance, refund / cashback / rebate (linked to the original fuel, toll, maintenance … or bank debit), reversal,
     internal transfer, or classify and record as other income. Overpayment only when ticked.
   * **Debits**: match fuel/toll/maintenance/tyre/insurance/tax/permit/PESO/vendor invoices (select several items to
     settle a bulk statement), internal transfer, reversal, or **Allocate / split** to cost centers with expense types
     (totals must equal the amount; over-allocation is refused). Asset purchase = allocation with a capex expense type.
   * **Suggested matches** are ranked (amount, UTR, invoice/contract number, party keywords, dates) — accept or reject.
   * **Keep unmatched** (with a note) or **Ignore**; everything unresolved stays visible on the dashboard.
3. Unmatch / reverse with a reason at any time; the reconciliation history and audit log record every step.
   Allocations/matches above configured amounts go to **Approvals**.

## 6. Fuel & toll

Imports populate the same tables as manual entry. Fuel rows are validated for quantity × rate ≈ amount (tolerance),
fuel type vs vehicle configuration, odometer chronology and efficiency (KM/L, KM/KG, KM/kWh; unusual values are
flagged, not labelled fraud). Toll rows are matched to vehicles (mappings/FASTag/registration) and plazas.
Unmatched/flagged rows appear in **Toll Review** / fuel status filters — **Resolve** assigns the vehicle/plaza and can
remember the mapping for future imports. Cost centers follow the vehicle as at the transaction date.

**Toll plazas from the internet:** **Toll → Toll Plazas → Fetch from Internet** adds and updates plazas with
**toll ID, toll plaza name, place and state** (all compulsory — records without them are skipped and listed).
Choose the source (OpenStreetMap is free and ready; data.gov.in needs a free API key), tick the states (none = all of
India) and optionally *Dry run* first. Every run is kept in **Toll → Toll Plaza Internet Sync**. Schedule
`toll_plazas_sync.bat` weekly to keep the list current. Details: `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md`.

**Viewing and updating toll plazas:** **Toll → Toll Plaza Directory & Map** shows the totals, a state-wise count and
a map; search or click a state, then **View / Update** opens the plaza in the register. In **Toll → Toll Plazas**
click any row to change its details and press **Update**. Changing a fetched plaza ticks **Keep My Changes**, so
later internet fetches do not overwrite your correction (untick it to allow updates again). Each plaza also links to
Google Maps and can re-fetch its own state; ⋯ → **Audit history** shows every change.

## 7. Maintenance

Job cards with parts, labour and other costs (totals computed), odometer (validated), downtime, vendor/workshop,
next due km/date from the maintenance type, approval for large amounts, **Complete** / **Cancel**, documents.

## 8. Tyres

* **Tyre Master**: purchase details → the tyre enters stock at the chosen location.
* Lifecycle actions (from the tyre row, the VSB bar or the **Tyre Dashboard**): Install (vehicle + position +
  odometer), Shift position (optional swap), Transfer to another vehicle, Remove (to godown/workshop/…; running km =
  removal − installation odometer), Move location, Send/Receive retreading (retread count increases only here),
  Inspection, Tyre maintenance, Warranty claim → Resolve, Scrap, Sell/Lost, audited Correction.
* **Tyre Dashboard**: pick a vehicle to see its configured positions with serial, brand, size, tread, fitment date,
  odometers and running km; click a tyre for its complete timeline and cost per km; click an empty position to install.

## 9. Reports & exports

**Reports → Report Centre**: Vehicle profitability / cost center (income, fuel, toll, maintenance, tyres, insurance,
tax, permit, PESO, driver, other, allocated common costs, net contribution, cost/km), category costs (history-aware),
ledger, income, receivables, fuel efficiency & summary, toll summary & review, maintenance, tyre master/stock/
movements/cost per km/brand performance/retreading/warranty/inspection/scrap, compliance register, bank
reconciliation & unmatched, vendor spend, vehicle classification history, audit, **trip profitability** (freight vs
fuel and toll in the trip window, trip expenses and driver allowance), LR register, POD pending, e-way bill register,
driver advances, driver settlements, e-invoice status. Filters by date range, branch,
vehicle, category, sub-category, cost center type, customer, provider, status; export to Excel, CSV, PDF or print.

## 10. Alerts, approvals, audit

* Bell icon → unread alerts (renewals, insurance, permits, fitness, PUCC, PESO, licences, unmatched bank, import
  errors, unusual fuel, tyre tread/warranty, maintenance due, pending approvals, e-way bills expiring / expired in
  transit, POD pending, unsettled driver advances). Configure channels in Notification Rules.
* **Approvals** inbox: approve/reject with remarks; multi-level where configured; self-approval blocked by default.
* **Audit Log**: every create/update (field diff)/delete/import/match/unmatch/allocation/approval/override/renewal/
  tyre movement/cost-center change with user, time, IP and reason.
