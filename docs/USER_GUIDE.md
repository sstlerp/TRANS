# User guide (workflows)

All screens share one layout: a dark toolbar with the screen title and **New / Edit / Save / Close**, a sticky
summary bar (VSB) for the open record with its status and quick actions, a sectioned form, and the list below with
search (partial or *exact*), filters, date range, sorting (click headers), pagination and Excel/CSV/PDF/print.
Row menu (⋯): actions, **Documents**, **Audit history**, activate/deactivate, delete (soft). Dates are typed and shown
as **DD/MM/YYYY** (time as HH:MM).

## 1. First-time setup (Administration & masters)

1. **Organization** → Companies, Branches, Departments, Locations (godowns, workshops, warehouses), States/Districts/RTOs.
2. **Administration → Master Configuration (Lookups)**: adjust dropdown values (vehicle types/statuses, ownership,
   document types, contract types, insurance policy types …).
3. **Fleet → Vehicle Categories / Sub-categories / Sub-category Attributes** (e.g. Truck → LPG with Tank Capacity,
   Axle Count …). **Cost Centers**: add non-vehicle ones (Administration, Workshop, Common …).
4. **Tyres → Positions / Vehicle Tyre Layouts / Locations**; **Fuel → Fuel Types / Stations / Cards**;
   **Toll → Plazas**; **Finance → Banks / Bank Accounts / Credit Types / Expense Types / Matching Rules**.
5. **Compliance → Renewal Types / Renewal Rules** (e.g. PESO for LPG sub-category, 2 certificates).
6. **Administration → System Settings / Rules**: tolerances, reminder days, odometer policy, partial allocation,
   income basis, common-cost allocation basis, tyre cost treatment. **Approval / Notification rules**, **Users & Roles**.

## 2. Vehicles

*New* → identity, category + sub-category (technical attributes appear for the chosen sub-category), branch, make,
status, capacity, tyre layout, opening odometer, **Fuel Configuration** (one or more fuel types, one primary).
On save the vehicle's **cost center** is created and renewal slots are generated from the renewal rules.
Category/sub-category/cost center are locked afterwards: use **Change Category / Cost Center** with an effective date
and reason — history is preserved and reports use the classification valid on each transaction date.
**Record Odometer** logs readings with chronology checks (lower readings need an authorised override with reason).

## 3. Compliance & insurance

* **Renewal Dashboard / Calendar**: due today / ≤7 / ≤15 / ≤30 days, expired, renewed, pending documents/payment,
  per category tabs and a month calendar.
* **Renewals**: one record per certificate; multiple slots per vehicle (e.g. *PESO #1*, *PESO #2*). Use **Renew** to
  create the next certificate — the old one becomes RENEWED and stays in history. Attach the certificate via Documents.
* **Insurance**: several concurrent policies per vehicle (vehicle, driver, CLL, PLI …), **Renew Policy** keeps history.
* Reminders are generated by the daily job at the configured days (per rule/slot or the global default) — see Alerts.

## 4. Contracts, invoices & receipts

Contracts hold customer, dates, billing method/rate and **vehicle allocation %**. Invoices (incl. partial, advance,
credit note) must allocate their taxable income to vehicles/cost centers (auto-filled from the contract split).
Income is recognised from invoices; bank receipts only settle them (Receivables report shows ageing).

## 5. Bank statements & reconciliation

1. Import the statement (Imports → Import Wizard, type BANK, choose the account) or add a manual transaction.
2. Every credit starts **UNCLASSIFIED**. Open **Finance → Reconciliation Workbench**:
   * **Credits**: match invoice(s) (partial, many invoices, or several receipts for one invoice), contract income /
     advance, refund / cashback / rebate (linked to the original fuel, toll, maintenance … or bank debit), reversal,
     internal transfer, or classify and record as other income. Overpayment only when ticked.
   * **Debits**: match fuel/toll/maintenance/tyre/insurance/tax/permit/PESO/vendor invoices (select several items to
     settle a bulk statement), internal transfer, reversal, or **Allocate / split** to cost centers with expense types
     (totals must equal the amount; over-allocation is refused). Asset purchase = allocation with a capex expense type.
   * **Suggested matches** are ranked (amount, UTR, invoice/contract number, party keywords, dates) — accept or reject.
   * **Keep unmatched** (with a note) or **Ignore**; everything unresolved stays visible on the dashboard.
3. Unmatch / reverse with a reason at any time; the reconciliation history and audit log record every step.
   Allocations/matches above configured amounts go to **Approvals**.

## 6. Fuel & toll

Imports populate the same tables as manual entry. Fuel rows are validated for quantity × rate ≈ amount (tolerance),
fuel type vs vehicle configuration, odometer chronology and efficiency (KM/L, KM/KG, KM/kWh; unusual values are
flagged, not labelled fraud). Toll rows are matched to vehicles (mappings/FASTag/registration) and plazas.
Unmatched/flagged rows appear in **Toll Review** / fuel status filters — **Resolve** assigns the vehicle/plaza and can
remember the mapping for future imports. Cost centers follow the vehicle as at the transaction date.

## 7. Maintenance

Job cards with parts, labour and other costs (totals computed), odometer (validated), downtime, vendor/workshop,
next due km/date from the maintenance type, approval for large amounts, **Complete** / **Cancel**, documents.

## 8. Tyres

* **Tyre Master**: purchase details → the tyre enters stock at the chosen location.
* Lifecycle actions (from the tyre row, the VSB bar or the **Tyre Dashboard**): Install (vehicle + position +
  odometer), Shift position (optional swap), Transfer to another vehicle, Remove (to godown/workshop/…; running km =
  removal − installation odometer), Move location, Send/Receive retreading (retread count increases only here),
  Inspection, Tyre maintenance, Warranty claim → Resolve, Scrap, Sell/Lost, audited Correction.
* **Tyre Dashboard**: pick a vehicle to see its configured positions with serial, brand, size, tread, fitment date,
  odometers and running km; click a tyre for its complete timeline and cost per km; click an empty position to install.

## 9. Reports & exports

**Reports → Report Centre**: Vehicle profitability / cost center (income, fuel, toll, maintenance, tyres, insurance,
tax, permit, PESO, driver, other, allocated common costs, net contribution, cost/km), category costs (history-aware),
ledger, income, receivables, fuel efficiency & summary, toll summary & review, maintenance, tyre master/stock/
movements/cost per km/brand performance/retreading/warranty/inspection/scrap, compliance register, bank
reconciliation & unmatched, vendor spend, vehicle classification history, audit. Filters by date range, branch,
vehicle, category, sub-category, cost center type, customer, provider, status; export to Excel, CSV, PDF or print.

## 10. Alerts, approvals, audit

* Bell icon → unread alerts (renewals, insurance, permits, fitness, PUCC, PESO, licences, unmatched bank, import
  errors, unusual fuel, tyre tread/warranty, maintenance due, pending approvals). Configure channels in Notification Rules.
* **Approvals** inbox: approve/reject with remarks; multi-level where configured; self-approval blocked by default.
* **Audit Log**: every create/update (field diff)/delete/import/match/unmatch/allocation/approval/override/renewal/
  tyre movement/cost-center change with user, time, IP and reason.
